About RugbyPro

RugbyPro Equipment Store is a retail business that provides high-quality rugby equipment and accessories for players, clubs, schools, and supporters across South Africa.

Our History

RugbyPro was founded in 2015 by a small group of former club players who saw a gap in the market for reliable, affordable rugby gear. What started as a single store in Johannesburg has grown into a trusted supplier serving clubs and schools nationwide.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide reliable and affordable rugby equipment that helps players improve their performance, safety, and enjoyment of the sport.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become the most trusted rugby equipment supplier in South Africa, known for quality products and excellent customer service.

Our Values

  • Quality products
  • Customer satisfaction
  • Supporting rugby development
  • Providing reliable equipment
